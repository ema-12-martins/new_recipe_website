# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction
Warm and homey, like a rustic café or bakery menu: kraft or chalkboard tones, cream paper,
terracotta and olive accents, a characterful display face for titles, and a handwritten touch
for small notes. Decoration must never cost readability: ingredient amounts and steps stay in a
clean, highly readable font, comfortable on a phone while cooking.

## Reference Points
- Rustic café / bakery menu boards (chalk, kraft paper, hand-drawn touches)
- Handwritten family recipe index cards
