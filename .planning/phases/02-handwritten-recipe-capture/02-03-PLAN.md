| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-02-15 | Tampering | lost update between members | medium | mitigate | photo changes apply only when the versioned text update matches; conflict applies nothing |
| T-02-16 | Information Disclosure | photos left on a shared device | high | mitigate | photos store cleared in the same transaction as recipes on sign-out, account switch and lost access; e2e asserts empty store |

| T-02-18 | Denial of Service | old cached reader vs new database version | medium | mitigate | STATIC_CACHE bump and constants test keep reader and app on one schema |
